# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time

Orders are confirmed immediately when payment is captured and stock is reserved.
An order stays in PENDING status while we verify it, and moves to PROCESSING
automatically within 5 minutes. Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are
usually picked the same day.

## Delivery Estimates

- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 working days after dispatch.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 working days after dispatch.
- Next-day delivery: ordered before 2pm, delivered the next working day.

Delivery estimates start from the moment the order is DISPATCHED, not from when
it was placed. Working days exclude weekends and public holidays.

## Tracking Your Order

Once an order reaches SHIPPED status, a tracking number is emailed to the
address on the order. You can also check the current status at any time using
your order number, which begins with ORD-.

## Delayed Orders

If an order has not arrived within 10 working days of dispatch, we treat it as
delayed. Contact support with your order number and we will open an
investigation with the carrier. Investigations take up to 5 working days.
If a parcel is confirmed lost, we will either replace the item or issue a full
refund, whichever you prefer.

## Shipping Costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over 50.00 USD. Below that, standard
delivery costs 4.99 USD. Express delivery costs 9.99 USD and next-day costs
14.99 USD regardless of order value.
