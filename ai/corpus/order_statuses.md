# Order Statuses Explained

## PENDING

We have received your order and payment authorisation, and it is waiting to be
picked. This is the only status in which you can cancel the order yourself.
Orders normally leave PENDING within 5 minutes.

## PROCESSING

Stock has been reserved for your order and your payment has been captured. The
warehouse is picking and packing the items. You can no longer cancel the order
yourself at this point; contact support if you need to change it.

## SHIPPED

Your order has left the warehouse and is with the carrier. A tracking number
has been emailed to you. Delivery estimates start from this point.

## DELIVERED

The carrier has confirmed delivery. This is a final status and will not change.
Your 30-day return window starts from this date.

## CANCELLED

The order was cancelled, either by you or by support on your behalf. Any payment
taken has been refunded and reserved stock has been released. This is a final
status.

## FAILED

We could not complete the order. The most common reasons are that the payment
was declined or that an item turned out to be unavailable after all. Nothing is
charged for a FAILED order, and anything already taken is refunded
automatically. The order page shows the specific reason. This is a final status.

## Status Order

The normal sequence is PENDING, then PROCESSING, then SHIPPED, then DELIVERED.
An order can only move forwards through these, never backwards. CANCELLED and
FAILED are endpoints that can be reached from PENDING or PROCESSING.
