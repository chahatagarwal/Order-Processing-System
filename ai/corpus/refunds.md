# Refunds Policy

## How Long Refunds Take

Refunds are issued within 5 working days of being approved. Once we have issued
the refund, your bank or card provider typically takes a further 3 to 5 working
days to make the money available. The total time is therefore usually 8 to 10
working days.

## Refund Method

Refunds always go back to the original payment method used for the order. We
cannot refund to a different card or account. If your card has expired, the
refund still reaches the account behind it in almost all cases; if it does not,
your bank will contact you.

## Partial Refunds

If you return part of an order, we refund the price of the returned items plus
any shipping you paid specifically for them. The remaining items are unaffected.

## What Is Refunded

- The price paid for the item is always refunded.
- Original shipping is refunded if the item was faulty, damaged or incorrect.
- Original shipping is not refunded if you simply changed your mind.
- Return postage is paid by us for faulty items, and by you otherwise.

## Refunds for Damaged or Faulty Items

If an item arrives damaged or faulty, you are entitled to a full refund
including all shipping costs, or a replacement, whichever you prefer. Report it
within 30 days of delivery and include a photograph if you can. We do not
require you to return a damaged item before we issue the refund.
