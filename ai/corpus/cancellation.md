# Cancellation Policy

## When You Can Cancel

You can cancel an order yourself while it is still in PENDING status. PENDING
means we have received the order but have not yet started picking it in the
warehouse. In practice this gives you about 5 minutes after placing the order.

Use the Cancel button on the order page, or contact support with your order
number. Cancellation is immediate and free.

## Once Processing Has Started

Once an order moves to PROCESSING status it can no longer be cancelled
directly, because stock has been reserved and the payment has been captured.
Contact support as soon as possible: if the order has not yet been dispatched
we can often still stop it, but we cannot guarantee it.

## Once Shipped

An order in SHIPPED status cannot be cancelled. You will need to refuse the
delivery or return the item once it arrives. See the returns policy.

## Refunds After Cancellation

When an order is cancelled, any payment that was captured is refunded
automatically to the original payment method. Refunds are issued within 5
working days, and your bank may take a further 3 to 5 working days to show it.
Reserved stock is released back to inventory immediately.

## Orders That We Cancel

Occasionally we have to cancel an order ourselves, for example if an item turns
out to be unavailable or if the payment is declined. An order cancelled by us
shows the status FAILED along with the reason. Nothing is charged, and any
amount already taken is refunded in full.
