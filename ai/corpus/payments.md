# Payments Policy

## When You Are Charged

Your card is authorised when you place the order and captured when the order is
confirmed, which normally happens within seconds. You will see one charge for
the order total.

## Declined Payments

If a payment is declined, the order is marked FAILED and nothing is taken. The
most common reasons are insufficient funds, an expired card, or your bank's
fraud checks. Your bank will usually tell you which. You can place the order
again with a different payment method.

## Duplicate Charges

You will never be charged twice for one order. Every payment request carries an
idempotency key, so if your connection drops and the request is retried, the
same key is recognised and only one charge is made.

If you see two charges for what you believe is one order, one of them is almost
always a pending authorisation that has not yet cleared and will disappear
within a few working days. Contact support with your order number if it does
not.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. We do not currently accept
cheques, bank transfers or cash on delivery.

## Currency

All prices are shown and charged in US dollars. If your card is in another
currency, your bank converts it at their rate and may add a fee.
